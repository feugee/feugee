# YouTube embeds without consent gating

Videos on the public site can be Embedded Videos — external videos, YouTube today, referenced by URL instead of uploaded as Assets — so long agency videos don't hit the 100 MB upload cap or R2 storage costs. They play ambient (muted, looping, controls-free) exactly like uploaded videos, initialized only in viewport via the YouTube IFrame API against `youtube-nocookie.com`, falling back to their Poster on player error. (An original reduced-motion fallback — poster in place of the player — was removed by ADR 0011.) Their Poster is the provider's thumbnail, ingested into `assets` once on save, so nothing touches Google's servers until a player mounts.

The embed deliberately loads without consent gating: a never-touched nocookie embed sets no cookies and measures nothing, ambient playback offers no click to serve as intent, and gating it would reduce the hero and cards to static posters for every visitor who declines. (The site's consent-gated Google Tag Manager experiment — the contrast this decision was made against — has since been reverted; the reasoning stands should consent gating return.) If the privacy bar ever becomes "zero Google contact without opt-in", the gate must be added at the player, not the CMS.
