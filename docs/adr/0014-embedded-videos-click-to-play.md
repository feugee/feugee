# Embedded Videos click-to-play in a Work's content surfaces

Embedded Videos are interactive in a Work's content surfaces — the Work Detail Page's hero and its Layout Items — and ambient everywhere else. Interactive means the visitor starts the video from its Poster, with sound, and drives it with the site's own controls (play/pause, mute, seek, time — custom-built over the IFrame API, YouTube's chrome kept out of the design language), the controls auto-hide during playback, only one video plays at a time per page, and a finished video stops on its final frame for a replay instead of looping. Everywhere decorative — the Hero's Slides and the Works' card Thumbnails — Embedded Videos keep the ambient muted-looping behavior of ADR 0010, and so do uploaded video Assets on every surface.

The asymmetry between the two arms of the Video Source either/or is deliberate: embedded videos are the Agency's content videos, where deliberate sound beats surprise sound — so playback starts on click rather than autoplaying muted until unmuted — while uploaded Assets are the site's ambient motion. Full parity between Assets and embeds was considered and rejected; a video uploaded as an Asset plays as a silent loop even where the same video embedded from YouTube would be click-to-play, and that is the recorded intent, not an oversight. So is the placement split: the Hero's autoplaying slider and the muted-looping card Thumbnails are decorative by design, and a seek bar over either would fight them.
